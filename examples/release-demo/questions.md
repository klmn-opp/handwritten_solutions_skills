# Public CPU demonstration

These original demonstration questions contain no personal or historical homework data.

## 1. A symmetric matrix

Let A have rows (2, 1) and (1, 2), and let b=(3, 1)^T. Find the determinant,
inverse, and solution of Ax=b. Find an orthonormal eigenbasis and verify a spectral
decomposition of A.

## 2. An integral and its error bound

Evaluate the integral of x^2/(1+x) from zero to one. Find an antiderivative and
check it by differentiation. Use 1 <= 1+x <= 2 to give a simple pair of bounds
for the integral and verify that the exact answer lies between them.
