For divisor $d$, the multiples below 1000 are $d,2d,\ldots,nd$, where $n$ is the integer part of $999/d$. Their sum is

$$ S_d=d\frac{n(n+1)}{2}. $$

There are 333 multiples of 3, 199 multiples of 5, and 66 multiples of 15. Subtract the multiples of 15 once because both sums include them.

$$ S_3+S_5-S_{15}=166833+99500-33165=233168. $$
