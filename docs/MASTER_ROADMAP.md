block reliable CPU product work. Never promote a candidate on update count,
parameter count, test count, reconstruction quality or selected seeds alone.

unseen university homework or unseen author scans. Old four/seven pages remain
regression. Once a holdout is consumed, fixes use development/regression and new
independence must be established explicitly.
